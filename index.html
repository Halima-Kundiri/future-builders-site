What is Not Too Young?

Why wait until university
to learn the big stuff?

Not Too Young is a hands-on learning programme for curious young minds in Abuja, and no one is too young to start. We take topics people call "too complicated for children", in any subject, and turn them into something kids can see, touch, build and pitch. Chapter 1, Future Builders, explored the human heart. Next time, it could be something completely different. See what's next at Not Too Young →

Why Not Too Young?

Not another lecture.
A day they'll talk about for weeks.

Not Too Young isn't a science class or a single subject. Every chapter takes on a new big topic and turns it into something kids can see, touch and build. Here's what your child gets:

🙌

They learn by doing

Making, testing, building and playing. Every session is hands-on, so the learning actually sticks.

🧠

Big topics, made simple

Any subject, even the "too complicated" ones, broken down into words kids understand and activities they love.

💡

They think like inventors

Kids spot real problems and design their own fixes, from wellness cafés to wildfire sensors.

🎤

Confidence to speak up

Kids ask questions, share ideas and, on some Chapters, pitch to the room for real feedback. Shy kids leave standing taller.

🤝

Teamwork & new friends

Kids work in small teams, so every child is seen, heard and helped by a facilitator.

🏅

Proud of what they made

Their painted hearts, their team posters and their very own idea up on the Wall of Inventions.

Meet the founder

Halima Kundiri

I'm a final-year Biotechnology student at Baze University, and I started Not Too Young because I believe children learn best when they get to touch, build and question, not just listen.

I want kids in Abuja to discover early that no topic is too big for them, whether it's the human body, business, technology or the arts, and that noticing a problem and building a fix is how every great invention, and every great business, begins.

  • 🧬Final-year Biotechnology student, Baze University
  • 💛Community educator: helped run the BD Pad Project, rallying 100+ donors for menstrual health education in Abuja and Kano
  • 📣Social media & brand strategist working in digital marketing: I know how to make big ideas exciting and easy to understand
  • 🎨Creative and innovator who loves turning ideas into things people can use
"Understand how something works, and you can invent ways to make it better."
